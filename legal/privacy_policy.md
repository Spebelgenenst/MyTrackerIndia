## 3. Data We Do **Not** Collect
I **do not** directly collect, store, or process:
- Your **password** (temporarily processed **only** for MyPayIndia authentication and **never stored** by the Project).
- **Real-world personal data** (e.g., government ID, address, phone number, email).
- **Payment card details** or other financial information.
- **Any data beyond what MyPayIndia’s API provides**.

---

## 4. Data We **Do** Collect
To function, the Project **automatically** collects and stores the following **from MyPayIndia’s API**:
- **Username** (as provided by MyPayIndia).
- **Session ID** (a permanent token for API access. You can invalidate the session on the [MyPayIndia webpage.](https://mypayindia.com)).
- **Balance** (your MyPayIndia account balance in **fictional currency**).
- **Balance History** (timestamps and balance updates).
- **Date of the MyPayIndia Account creation** (the exact time and date, where the MyPayIndia account was created)
- **Technical Data** (e.g., IP addresses via Flask logs, which are standard for web servers).

> **Note:** All data originates **solely from MyPayIndia’s API**. I **do not** modify, verify, or endorse its accuracy.

- Display your **balance and statistics** in the Project.
